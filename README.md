# Three-Statement-Financial-Model
Fully linked three-statement financial model of Vertiv Holdings (NYSE: VRT) built in Excel, with FY2023–25 historicals and a FY2026–29 forecast.

## What's in the model

- Historical financials for FY2023 to FY2025
- Forecast for FY2026 to FY2029
- Supporting schedules for PP&E, goodwill and intangibles, working capital and debt
- A balance sheet that balances in every forecast year

## How I built the forecast

Each assumption has a documented source. I used management guidance, historical ratios, analyst consensus and first-half seasonality to set revenue growth, margins and other inputs. Working capital is forecast using receivable, inventory and payable days. Interest expense uses a weighted average rate across the company's floating and fixed rate debt.

## Key outputs

- Net sales forecast to grow from $10.2bn in FY2025 to $25.1bn in FY2029
- Net debt of $1.1bn in FY2025 moving to a net cash position by FY2026

## Why Vertiv

This model follows on from my research on data center and AI infrastructure, where I identified power and cooling as the most attractive part of the supply chain.
